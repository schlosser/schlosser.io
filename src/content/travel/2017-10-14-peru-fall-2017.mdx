After a somewhat complicated series of flights that involved an unexpected overnight layover in Mexico City (thanks, Interjet) we arrived in Cusco. It was immediately apparent that we were at a much higher altitude, over 11,000 feet. A quick check-in to our hotel and we were off to explore the city.

<section class="text">
<h2 id="cusco">Cusco</h2>

Cusco is a beautiful city, with a mix of Spanish colonial architecture and older Incan stonework. We spent our first day wandering around, acclimatizing to the altitude, and drinking lots of coca tea.

<Image image={import("img/travel/2017-10-14-peru-fall-2017/cusco-1@2x.jpg")} caption="Plaza de Armas, Cusco." withViewer />
<Image image={import("img/travel/2017-10-14-peru-fall-2017/cusco-2@2x.jpg")} caption="Street scene in Cusco." withViewer />
<Image image={import("img/travel/2017-10-14-peru-fall-2017/cusco-3@2x.jpg")} caption="Incan stonework visible everywhere." withViewer />

The next day we took a tour of the Sacred Valley. Our first stop was Chinchero, a small village known for its weaving traditions.

<Image image={import("img/travel/2017-10-14-peru-fall-2017/chinchero-1@2x.jpg")} caption="Chinchero market." withViewer />
<Image image={import("img/travel/2017-10-14-peru-fall-2017/chinchero-2@2x.jpg")} caption="Weaving demonstration." withViewer />

Then it was on to Ollantaytambo, a massive Incan fortress and town. The scale of the stonework here was incredible.

<Image image={import("img/travel/2017-10-14-peru-fall-2017/ollantaytambo-1@2x.jpg")} caption="Ollantaytambo." withViewer />
<Image image={import("img/travel/2017-10-14-peru-fall-2017/ollantaytambo-pano-1@2x.jpg")} caption="Panoramic view from Ollantaytambo." withViewer id="ollantaytambo-pano-1" />
<Image image={import("img/travel/2017-10-14-peru-fall-2017/ollantaytambo-2@2x.jpg")} caption="Detail of Incan stonework." withViewer />

From Ollantaytambo, we caught the train to Aguas Calientes, the town at the base of Machu Picchu.

</section>
<section class="text">
<h2 id="machu-picchu">Machu Picchu</h2>

The next morning, we were up before dawn to catch one of the first buses up to Machu Picchu. It was an incredible experience to see the sun rise over the mountains and illuminate the ancient city.

<section class="text">
<h2 id="lima">Lima</h2>

After Machu Picchu, we returned to Cusco for a night before flying to Lima. We only had a short time in Lima, but we made the most of it, exploring the Miraflores district and enjoying some amazing food.

<Image image={import("img/travel/2017-10-14-peru-fall-2017/lima-1@2x.jpg")} caption="Parque del Amor, Miraflores, Lima." withViewer />
<Image image={import("img/travel/2017-10-14-peru-fall-2017/lima-2@2x.jpg")} caption="Huaca Pucllana, an ancient adobe pyramid in the middle of Miraflores." withViewer />
<Image image={import("img/travel/2017-10-14-peru-fall-2017/lima-3@2x.jpg")} caption="Delicious Peruvian cuisine." withViewer />

Peru was an incredible trip, packed with history, culture, and stunning landscapes.

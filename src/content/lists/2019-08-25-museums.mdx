One of my favorite things to do, both at home and while traveling, is visiting
museums. As a kid, I hated them, because I always felt pressured to learn and
understand the story behind everything I saw. In fact, I sort of thought that
was the <em>point</em>. Over the years, I've found a new joy in just walking
through museums (especially art museums) aimlessly. And I love it! This is a
running collection of my favorites, roughly ranked:
